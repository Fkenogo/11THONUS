## 5. Authority Analysis

**Relationship among TRD8, TRD10, and the Engineering Blueprint:**
- **TRD10 §10.5**: authoritative normative contract (established `RES-005.2a`, unchanged by this task, not reopened).
- **Engineering Blueprint §3.3**: engineering expression of that contract, self-declared subordinate to any disagreeing TRD chapter (§0), corrected `RES-005.2a`.
- **TRD8 §8.7**: a small, secondary section within a broad platform-architecture chapter (Part II) whose own primary subject — Firebase service responsibilities, project structure, event architecture — is not data-shape definition. TRD10 (Part III) is the dedicated data-architecture chapter whose entire purpose is defining Firestore document shapes.

**Is TRD8 intended to define, summarise, reference, or extend?** Direct inspection shows TRD8 §8.7's pre-correction text was a bare field-name list with no reasoning, no distinguishing content, and no scope narrower or broader than TRD10 §10.5's own shape — it duplicated rather than summarised, extended, or referenced. The Blueprint's own docstring (pre-`RES-005.2a`) cited "(TRD8 §8.7)" as its source, confirming the Blueprint's stale text was a direct transcription of TRD8's, not an independent drift. TRD8's proper role, given its own chapter scope, is the platform-level *acknowledgment* that documents carry shared metadata (a pattern statement) — not the field-level contract itself, which belongs in TRD10.

**Is there a genuinely independent TRD8 requirement conflicting with TRD10?** No. Every field, type, and semantic in TRD8 §8.7's pre-correction text matched the Blueprint's own pre-correction text exactly (`version`, non-nullable `createdBy`/`updatedBy`, `deletedAt`/`deletedBy`, `languageCode`, no `currencyCode`/`timezone`) — the same four differences from TRD10 that `RES-005.2a` already resolved. Nothing in TRD8 §8.7 was unique to TRD8. This is not a stop-and-report case.

## 6. Exact TRD8 Contradiction

TRD8 §8.7 ("Firestore Document Standards") stated, verbatim (pre-correction): a field list of `id`, `createdAt`, `createdBy`, `updatedAt`, `updatedBy`, `status`, `version`, and (where applicable) `businessId`, `customerId`, `countryCode`, `languageCode`, `deletedAt`, `deletedBy` — identical to the Blueprint's own pre-`RES-005.2a` text, and identical to all four differences from TRD10 §10.5 already catalogued in the `RES-005.2a` Contract Analysis (field naming, nullability, field naming+semantics, field presence).

## 7. Correction Strategy

**Chosen: cross-reference, not restatement** (Stage C option 2). Rationale: this exact duplication — a second, independently-maintained copy of the same shape in a different chapter — is the structural root cause of this entire three-task correction chain (`ENG-P2-000A` finding → `RES-005.2a` → `RES-005.2a-R1`). Restating the corrected shape a second time in TRD8 would recreate the identical divergence risk the moment either copy is next edited without the other. A cross-reference makes TRD10 §10.5 the single point of maintenance and structurally forecloses a third divergence, satisfying the task's own "choose the approach that best prevents future divergence" instruction.
