## 12. Mandatory-vs-recommended methodology

**Corrected by `-CORR-001` — see the Correction Pass below.** The original methodology incorrectly gated classification A on "a named LEG-FD item or Founder disposition itself" stating a jurisdiction requirement — this made *Founder repetition* a precondition for recognizing a mandatory statute, when a Founder disposition is a governance-architecture/product-policy position reconciling *discretionary* legal recommendations against Founder authority, not the source that makes a *mandatory* statute mandatory (the Founder Legal Architecture Disposition Record's own text states these are "governance-architecture and product-policy positions... not, by themselves, a legal conclusion"). The corrected methodology:

A topic is classified **A (Mandatory Overlay)** where the repository's reconciled legal-evidence chain — the Reconciliation Matrix, read together with the External Legal Opinion passage it reconciles and cites — identifies a specific, named mandatory statute or statutory provision applicable to the Business Terms for that jurisdiction, **whether or not a separate LEG-FD item independently restates it**, provided no later Founder disposition qualifies or rejects that specific point (in which case the qualification controls, per this task's own instruction not to treat the Opinion as authority where a Founder disposition qualifies or rejects it). A topic is classified **B (Optional/Recommended)** where the Opinion or Reconciliation Matrix recommends jurisdiction-specific content on a discretionary basis (not tied to a named mandatory statutory provision), and adopting it would not contradict any Founder disposition. A topic is classified **C (No Overlay Required)** only where a named authority **affirmatively demonstrates embodiment** — a LEG-FD item, `DEC-LOY-011`, or a Reconciliation Matrix row that expressly reconciles a jurisdiction-specific statutory or legal point and concludes the existing portable text already satisfies or is not contradicted by it — not merely because no contrary jurisdiction-specific authority happens to have been found. Absence of a contrary authority is not affirmative embodiment. A topic is classified **D (Unresolved)** wherever the repository contains no authority, conflicting authority, only a discretionary (non-mandatory) recommendation not yet reconciled to a decision addressing the Business Terms specifically, or only an absence of evidence either way — the precise wording used throughout this report for that last case is `D — UNRESOLVED / no additional overlay requirement currently established`, which is expressly **not** the same finding as an affirmative "no overlay is required" (C).

**Revised mandatory-overlay finding (was: "zero mandatory overlays exist"):** applying this corrected methodology to the full re-audited matrix at §12A, **one topic — Burundi pre-acceptance operator/role-separation/data-processing disclosure categories — is supported by reconciled, statute-named primary authority and is classified A**, not zero. The overall finding is restated in narrower, evidence-qualified terms at §26 below: no *additional* mandatory Business-Terms overlay requirement, beyond that one item, has currently been established from sufficiently verified repository-backed primary legal authority — this is not the same claim as an affirmative finding that no further overlay is required.
