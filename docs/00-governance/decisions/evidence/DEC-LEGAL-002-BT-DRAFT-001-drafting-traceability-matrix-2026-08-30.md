# Purpose

Every substantive clause in Part I of the Core Business Terms draft must be traceable to a governing authority. This matrix records that traceability at clause level. Per repository convention (confirmed by inspection: the Core Business Terms draft text itself does not embed internal decision-ID citations — LEG-FD-xx, FD-x, DEC-xxx-xxx strings do not appear inside the drafted clause text of comparable prior instruments), decision IDs are kept out of the contractual text itself and recorded here instead.

Legend — **Portable/Jurisdiction-dependent:** whether the clause is intended as portable Layer-1 language or is expected to vary by jurisdictional overlay. **External evidence:** whether the external Legal Opinion informed the clause (as accepted, qualified, or declined per the Reconciliation Matrix). **Unresolved input:** cross-reference to the Controlled Inputs Register where applicable.

## Clauses removed or not drafted after traceability review

None of the drafted clauses (§§1–7) were removed during the original v1.0 self-review — each traced to at least one governing authority or was explicitly marked as a controlled input where no authority existed. See the [Drafting Report](../../../05-implementation/reports/DEC-LEGAL-002-BT-DRAFT-001-drafting-report-2026-08-30.md) §14 for the full original clause-by-clause self-review and the prohibited-concept search results.

**Correction pass (`DEC-LEGAL-002-BT-DRAFT-001-CORR-001`):** former §4.4 (the service-tier-differentiation controlled-input marker) was removed from Part I in full, per Founder disposition — no governed service-tier differentiation exists, and the Core Terms are drafted to govern participation generally without inventing tiers. No other clause was removed; §1.2, §3.3, and §7.5 were resolved in place (rewritten, not removed) and §1.3, §3.2(c), §6.3, §7.1, §7.6, and the "11thONUS"/"the platform" definition were corrected in place. See the [Correction Report](../../../05-implementation/reports/DEC-LEGAL-002-BT-DRAFT-001-CORR-001-correction-report-2026-08-30.md) for the full clause-by-clause correction record.
