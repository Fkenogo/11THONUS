# DEC-LEGAL-002 Legal Counsel Question Set

Each question below is grounded in an actual unresolved issue found in the repository (cited). No question is pre-answered. This set is the working brief for the Burundi legal adviser named as `EXT-LEG-002`'s owner-adviser in the [External Dependencies Register](../external-dependencies-register.md).

**v2.0/v3.0 note:** items 5–8 below are revised so counsel is asked to advise on the *legal expression* of a Founder-approved product position (legality, enforceability, required wording, permitted exceptions, required notices, remedies, consumer protection), not to select the underlying product policy — the Founder has already done so (FD-2 through FD-6, and — as of v3.0 — the `DEC-LOY-011` default-redeemable-with-governed-exceptions resolution folded into item 5; see the [Founder Decision Sheet](DEC-LEGAL-002-PREP-001-founder-decision-sheet-2026-08-29.md) and the [Decision Register](../decision-register.md) `DEC-LOY-011` entry). Counsel remains free to flag that a recorded position is not legally viable as stated, in which case it returns to the Founder, not to counsel, to adjust the product position.

1. **Legal nature of the platform–business relationship.** 11thONUS provides shared infrastructure (identity, purchase verification, Verified Units, Terms acceptance) to independently operating businesses, each of which owns its own Reward Program and customer relationship (see [Product & Legal Decision Brief](DEC-LEGAL-002-PREP-001-product-legal-decision-brief-2026-08-29.md) §B.1, B.4). What is the correct legal characterisation of this relationship (platform/service-provider, agency, marketplace, other), and what does that characterisation imply for the Terms drafting?

2. **Legal nature of the platform–customer relationship.** The customer participates in a business's Reward Program via the platform, without a direct commercial relationship with 11thONUS itself (`DEC-PROD-004`, CONFIRMED: "customers never pay"). Is a direct Customer Terms/consent relationship with 11thONUS required, and if so, what is its legal basis?

3. **Enforceability/form of electronic acceptance.** The current implementation records Terms acceptance as `{ id, acceptingCustomerIdentityId, businessId, termsVersion, acceptedAt, languageCode, collectionMethod?, createdAt, schemaVersion }` via an explicit affirmative checkbox at the point a business submits for verification ([ENG-P3-002A report](../../../05-implementation/reports/ENG-P3-002A-business-onboarding-backend-read-transport-terms-foundation-implementation-report-2026-08-22.md) §7, §19). Is this record content and acceptance mechanism sufficient for enforceable electronic acceptance under applicable law?

4. **Required disclosures.** What disclosures (about the platform, the business relationship, data handling, fees, or the reward mechanism) are legally required at or before Terms acceptance?

5. **Business obligation to honour earned/available rewards, including the redemption-during-suspension model — legal expression of a decided position.** **Founder positions recorded:** (i) a Business's obligation to honour rewards validly earned survives the Business's own suspension or exit, subject to the applicable Reward Program terms and any circumstances where fulfilment is legally impossible or counsel advises different treatment; 11thONUS does not become the guarantor or fulfiller of that obligation (FD-2, FD-3, 2026-08-29, `DEC-LEGAL-002-FOUNDER-DISP-001`); and (ii) valid rewards earned before Business suspension remain **redeemable during suspension by default** — suspension may stop/restrict new loyalty activity (new earning, new Reward Programs, other applicable Business capabilities) without automatically preventing redemption of already-earned rewards; redemption may nevertheless be restricted, paused, or subject to additional review only where the specific suspension reason makes continued redemption inappropriate or unsafe (suspected fraud, security/integrity concerns, legal/regulatory requirements, disputed reward validity, or another governed exception); suspension arising solely from the Business's commercial relationship with 11thONUS (including subscription/payment status) must not by itself prevent redemption of otherwise valid earned rewards (`DEC-LOY-011`, CONFIRMED 2026-08-29, `DEC-LEGAL-002-FOUNDER-DISP-001`). **Asked of counsel:** what legal form should the honour-obligation take (Business Terms clause, Reward Program-level requirement, or both); is the default-redeemable-with-exceptions model enforceable as between the Business, the customer, and the platform; what does "legally impossible" fulfilment need to mean for that exception to be workable; what legally required exceptions, notices, and remedies apply to the fraud/security/integrity/legal-regulatory/disputed-validity exception categories; and what consumer-protection implications follow from restricting redemption under those exceptions? **Not asked:** which redemption model to adopt (throughout/grace-only/manual-review/blocked-until-reactivation) — the Founder has already selected default-redeemable-with-governed-exceptions; counsel is not asked to choose among the original options.

6. **Treatment of programme changes — legal expression of a decided position.** **Founder position recorded (FD-5, 2026-08-29):** a Business may change its Reward Program prospectively, but must not retrospectively remove or materially reduce a reward already earned under the rules in force when it was earned. **Asked of counsel:** what required notice, effective-timing rule, and permissible exceptions make this enforceable (e.g., unilateral-variation doctrine, prior-notice requirements under applicable consumer-protection law)? Not asked: whether prospective-only change is the right policy.

7. **Outstanding rewards on business exit or suspension — legal expression of a decided position.** **Founder positions recorded (FD-2 exit-adjacent, FD-3, 2026-08-29):** exit does not automatically extinguish rewards validly earned before the effective exit date; the Business remains responsible for outstanding earned obligations under its Reward Program and the Business Terms; 11thONUS is not the guarantor of those obligations. **Asked of counsel:** what operational treatment, reasonable fulfilment period, customer-notice requirements, exceptional circumstances, and legal remedies should the Terms specify to make this enforceable and clear? Not asked: whether exit should extinguish earned rewards.

8. **Dispute allocation.** No dispute-resolution mechanism (customer-vs-business, business-vs-platform) is currently governed (Business Obligation Matrix "Customer disputes," "Business disputes") — this remains a genuinely open product question the Founder has not dispositioned. What mechanism(s) should the Terms require, and must they differ for business-facing vs. customer-facing Terms?

9. **Platform liability.** See [Platform Responsibility Matrix](DEC-LEGAL-002-PREP-001-business-obligation-matrix-2026-08-29.md#platform-responsibility-matrix-phase-d) — every row is marked `LEGAL COUNSEL TO ADVISE`; no exclusion or limitation is proposed. What liability exposure does 11thONUS actually carry given the product boundary described in §B of the Decision Brief (platform does not control programme content, goods/service delivery, or the customer relationship)?

10. **Business liability.** Symmetric to (9): what liability should the Business Terms impose on a participating business, consistent with the same product boundary?

11. **Legally permissible limitation/exclusion provisions.** Given the jurisdiction(s) involved (Burundi pilot; platform operated from Rwanda per [DEC-LEGAL-006 evidence pack](../DEC-LEGAL-006-Cross-Border-Hosting-and-Data-Residency-Evidence-Pack.md)), what limitation-of-liability language is enforceable, and what is prohibited as against public policy or consumer-protection law?

12. **Governing law.** Given the Rwanda-operation/Burundi-pilot cross-border posture already documented for `DEC-LEGAL-006` (see cited evidence pack), what governing law should apply to (a) the Business Terms and (b) any Customer Terms, and could they differ?

13. **Jurisdiction/dispute-resolution mechanism.** What forum (courts, arbitration, other) is appropriate and enforceable for each Terms instrument, given the same cross-border posture?

14. **Required language(s) for Terms/acceptance.** The platform records a `languageCode` on each Terms-acceptance record and supports EN/FR localisation at the product level; Burundi's official languages include Kirundi and French. Is a specific language or set of languages legally required for enforceable consumer-facing Terms in Burundi?

15. **Version-change and reacceptance requirements.** The current implementation collapses a "previously accepted, now superseded" Terms version to the same "not accepted" UI/backend state, requiring fresh acceptance ([ENG-P3-002-UI-IMP-D review](../../../05-implementation/reports/eng-p3-002-ui-imp-d-review-report-2026-08-26.md) §5). Is this reacceptance-on-version-change behavior legally sufficient, and are there minimum notice requirements before a new version takes effect?

16. **Mandatory consumer-protection provisions.** Burundi's Loi n° 1/11 de 2009 provides general consumer protection extended to e-commerce (clear pre-purchase information, fraud/misleading-advertising protection), enforced by the CNCP (per the [DEC-LEGAL-006 evidence pack](../DEC-LEGAL-006-Cross-Border-Hosting-and-Data-Residency-Evidence-Pack.md) §2). What provisions does this (or other applicable consumer-protection law) mandate be included in Customer Terms or Reward Program disclosures?

17. **Differentiated treatment for business-facing vs. customer-facing Terms.** Given the platform boundary (business-as-paying-subscriber, customer-as-non-paying-participant per `DEC-PROD-004`), should the two instruments differ in form, disclosure, or enforceability standard, and if so how?

18. **Platform suspension grounds and process — legal expression of a decided position.** **Founder position recorded (FD-4, 2026-08-29):** 11thONUS may suspend or restrict a Business's participation where necessary to protect platform trust, security, integrity, compliance, or participants; no exhaustive list of grounds is recorded. **Asked of counsel:** what grounds, notice/process, consequences, and reinstatement/termination treatment are legally required or advisable for this to be enforceable and defensible against a wrongful-suspension claim? Not asked: whether the platform should have suspension authority at all.

19. **Reward monetary characterisation and required disclosures — legal expression of a decided position.** **Founder position recorded (FD-6, 2026-08-29):** a reward is a benefit under the applicable Business's Reward Program, not money or a stored cash balance held by 11thONUS, and not a general cash-withdrawal entitlement; unless a Reward Program expressly permits otherwise, rewards are not redeemable for cash. **Asked of counsel:** does this characterisation avoid financial-instrument, e-money, or gift-card-equivalent regulatory treatment under applicable law; what disclosures (if any) are required to state this characterisation to customers; and does the characterisation hold consistently across both the Business Terms and any Customer Terms? Not asked: whether rewards should carry cash value.

20. **General subscription-terms framework — scoped to structure only, per FD-7.** **Founder position recorded (FD-7, 2026-08-29):** `DEC-LEGAL-002` may establish the general contractual framework governing subscriptions, fees, billing, cancellation, and changes, without inventing or settling any specific `DEC-SUB-*` value (plan names, prices, billing intervals, staff limits, trial structure, complimentary/pilot plans, proration, grace periods, billing ownership, tiering). **Asked of counsel:** what general/structural subscription-terms language (parties, billing-cycle mechanics, cancellation rights, changes-to-terms) can be drafted now, independent of the specific commercial values, without requiring later rework once those values are confirmed? Not asked: any specific price, plan name, or billing value.

**Not answered here:** none of the above questions is pre-answered, and no draft clause is supplied. See the [Terms Content Architecture](DEC-LEGAL-002-PREP-001-terms-content-architecture-2026-08-29.md) for content-heading proposals only (not legal language) that these answers would populate.
