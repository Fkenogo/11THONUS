# DEC-LEGAL-002 Business Obligation Matrix

Reconstructs the product-governed obligations that may need legal expression. Where product governance is silent, the row is marked `PRODUCT DECISION REQUIRED` rather than filled with an invented rule. Where the answer depends on legal characterisation rather than product choice, it is marked `LEGAL CONFIRMATION NEEDED`. A row can carry both.

|---|---|---|---|
| Programme publication | A business configures and publishes its own Reward Program; no platform-level review or approval of programme content is documented. | [Product Manifesto](../../../01-product/11thONUS%20Product%20Manifesto.md) — business owns programme design | `PRODUCT DECISION REQUIRED` (does publication itself create a binding offer to customers?) — **LEGAL CONFIRMATION NEEDED** |
| Eligibility rules | Not governed beyond "one actively-accumulating Loyalty Cycle per customer per Reward Program at a time" (structural rule, not an eligibility policy). | [Verified Loyalty Principles](../../verified-loyalty-principles.md) | `PRODUCT DECISION REQUIRED` |

## Platform Responsibility Matrix (Phase D)

The governed boundary between 11thONUS's own responsibilities and a participating business's responsibilities. Where legal allocation of liability is required rather than a product-governance fact, the row says `LEGAL COUNSEL TO ADVISE` — no liability exclusion is invented here.

| TOPIC | PLATFORM RESPONSIBILITY (as governed today) | BUSINESS RESPONSIBILITY (as governed today) | LIABILITY ALLOCATION |
|---|---|---|---|
| Accuracy of business-created programme terms | None — the platform does not review or approve individual Reward Program content. | The business defines and is responsible for its own Reward Program's accuracy. | `LEGAL COUNSEL TO ADVISE` |
| Delivery of underlying goods/services | None — outside platform scope entirely; the platform does not sell or fulfil goods/services. | Full responsibility — the business is the seller/service provider. | `LEGAL COUNSEL TO ADVISE` |
| Honouring rewards | None — 11thONUS does not become the guarantor or fulfiller of a Business's reward obligations (FD-2, FD-3). | **Founder position recorded (FD-2):** the Business bears the obligation to honour rewards validly earned before suspension/exit, subject to the applicable Reward Program terms and any legally-impossible-fulfilment exception. | `LEGAL COUNSEL TO ADVISE` on redemption mechanism, exceptions, remedies |
| Customer service | Not governed as a platform responsibility in any source reviewed. | Implied by "business owns the customer relationship" (Product Manifesto), but not stated as a formal obligation. | `LEGAL COUNSEL TO ADVISE` |
| Commercial disputes (business's own commerce, unrelated to the platform) | None. | Full responsibility, by product-boundary principle. | `LEGAL COUNSEL TO ADVISE` |
| Platform record integrity | Platform-owned: Verified Units, Terms-acceptance records, purchase-recording infrastructure are platform-built and platform-maintained. | Business supplies the underlying transaction data via staff recording. | Not primarily a liability-allocation question; `LEGAL COUNSEL TO ADVISE` only on evidentiary weight of platform records in a dispute |
| Platform availability | Platform-owned (infrastructure/uptime), not detailed in sources reviewed for this brief. | None. | `LEGAL COUNSEL TO ADVISE` |
| Verification (business/customer identity) | Platform-owned mechanism (Capability 3 identity/staff work; `DEC-SUB-007` guarantees verification is never paywalled). | Business supplies the underlying identity information at registration/invitation. | `LEGAL COUNSEL TO ADVISE` on any residual liability for verification failures |
| Fraud response | Not governed as a platform responsibility in sources reviewed. | Not governed. | `PRODUCT DECISION REQUIRED`, then `LEGAL COUNSEL TO ADVISE` |
| Account suspension | **Founder position recorded (FD-4):** 11thONUS may suspend/restrict participation for governed trust, security, integrity, or compliance reasons; does not transfer the Business's customer-relationship/Reward Program responsibility to 11thONUS. | N/A | Product policy no longer open — `LEGAL COUNSEL TO ADVISE` on grounds, notice/process, consequences, reinstatement/termination, and required safeguards |
| Business verification | Platform-owned mechanism (part of the onboarding→verification flow, Capability 3). | Business supplies onboarding data. | None identified beyond general accuracy questions |
| Customer verification | Platform-owned mechanism, present in every plan (`DEC-SUB-007`). | N/A | None identified |
| Incorrect transactions | Not governed as a Terms-level obligation in sources reviewed (internal correction/audit mechanisms are an engineering concern, not confirmed as a customer-facing Terms right). | Not governed. | `PRODUCT DECISION REQUIRED`, then `LEGAL COUNSEL TO ADVISE` |
| Reward disputes | Not governed. | Not governed. | `PRODUCT DECISION REQUIRED`, then `LEGAL COUNSEL TO ADVISE` |

**No liability exclusion, allocation, or limitation is proposed anywhere in this matrix.** Every "LEGAL COUNSEL TO ADVISE" marking is a placeholder for counsel's judgment, not a pre-answered position. Rows updated above to reflect Founder dispositions record a **product/commercial position**, not a legal conclusion — the corresponding "LEGAL COUNSEL TO ADVISE" scope narrows to legal form, exceptions, process, and remedies, not to whether the underlying obligation exists at all.
