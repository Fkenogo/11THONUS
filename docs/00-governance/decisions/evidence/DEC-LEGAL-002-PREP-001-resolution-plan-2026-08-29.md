# DEC-LEGAL-002 Resolution Plan

The exact sequence required to move `DEC-LEGAL-002` from `OPEN_LEGAL` to resolved. This plan does not execute any step in the sequence — it only documents the sequence for the Founder to authorize step by step.

1. **Product position reconciliation** — ✅ **Complete as of 2026-08-29 (`DEC-LEGAL-002-FOUNDER-DISP-001`).** The Founder dispositioned the Founder Decision Sheet items FD-1 through FD-7: re-prioritising the Business Terms component now (FD-1); the earned-reward-obligation-survives-suspension position (FD-2); the earned-reward-obligation-survives-exit position (FD-3); the platform-suspension-authority position (FD-4); the prospective-only-programme-change position (FD-5); the rewards-are-not-cash position (FD-6); and the general-structural-subscription-terms-now position (FD-7). See the updated [Founder Decision Sheet](DEC-LEGAL-002-PREP-001-founder-decision-sheet-2026-08-29.md). **These are Founder product/commercial positions, not legal conclusions**, and `DEC-LEGAL-002` remains `OPEN_LEGAL`.
2. **Founder decisions where required** — ✅ **Complete for the `DEC-LOY-011` linkage, same task, 2026-08-29.** `DEC-LOY-011` (Decision Register) is now **CONFIRMED** — Option (a), redeemable by default during suspension, subject to governed exceptions — recorded per the [Decision Governance Workflow](../../decision-governance-workflow.md) under this explicit Founder instruction. Genuinely open product questions not covered by FD-1–FD-7 or `DEC-LOY-011` (e.g., programme publication, fraud/abuse, dispute mechanisms, `DEC-ID-005`'s broader owner-initiated-self-suspension question) remain outstanding.
3. **Legal counsel review** — the Burundi legal adviser (per `EXT-LEG-002`'s named owner-adviser) reviews the [Legal Counsel Question Set](DEC-LEGAL-002-PREP-001-legal-counsel-question-set-2026-08-29.md) (v2.0, reconciled against the FD-1–FD-7 dispositions) against the [Product & Legal Decision Brief](DEC-LEGAL-002-PREP-001-product-legal-decision-brief-2026-08-29.md) and the [DEC-LEGAL-002-FOUNDER-DISP-001 Legal Counsel Handoff Pack](DEC-LEGAL-002-FOUNDER-DISP-001-legal-counsel-handoff-pack-2026-08-29.md).
4. **Counsel advice/evidence filed** — counsel's answers are filed as the evidence record for `EXT-LEG-002` in the [External Dependencies Register](../external-dependencies-register.md), moving its status from `PENDING` toward `EVIDENCE_RECEIVED`, per that register's own rule: "No entry in this register may be closed by assumption."
5. **Founder acceptance** — per the [Decision Governance Workflow](../../decision-governance-workflow.md) §2 ("the founder accepts (never makes) legal advice for OPEN_LEGAL records"), the Founder reviews and accepts (or requests revision of) counsel's advice.
6. **`DEC-LEGAL-002` status update** — once accepted, the Decision Register entry is updated from `OPEN_LEGAL` to `CONFIRMED` (or another disposition), per the register's own amendment process (§5, decision-governance-workflow.md): a CONFIRMED decision is never edited in place; if the register entry needs correction later, a new record supersedes it. **This preparation task performs none of this step.**
7. **Approved Terms instrument/version** — the actual legal Terms content (Business Terms, and Customer Terms if determined necessary per Legal Counsel Question Set item 2) is drafted and approved, populating the headings proposed in the [Terms Content Architecture](DEC-LEGAL-002-PREP-001-terms-content-architecture-2026-08-29.md). A real version identifier is assigned.
8. **Engineering configuration authorization** — the Founder (or delegate) authorizes a single, explicit engineering action to write the approved version identifier to the `platformConfig/businessTerms` Firestore document's `currentVersion` field, per the existing, already-built mechanism described in [Terms Content Architecture](DEC-LEGAL-002-PREP-001-terms-content-architecture-2026-08-29.md) Phase G. **This preparation task does not perform this write.**
9. **End-to-end Terms acceptance/submission verification** — engineering verifies, against the newly configured version, that a real business can accept the current Terms and successfully call `submitBusinessForVerification` end to end (the one Capability 3 requirement currently marked ❌ in the [Capability 3 readiness report](../../../05-implementation/reports/eng-p3-002-closure-001-capability-3-readiness-report-2026-08-29.md)).
10. **Capability 3 closure assessment** — with step 9 verified, Capability 3's status is reassessed against the readiness report's requirements matrix and, if all rows are satisfied, Capability 3 closure is considered. **This preparation task does not close Capability 3 and does not authorize `ENG-P3-003` (Knowledge Studio), which is a separate, independent Founder decision per `DEC-CKS-002`.**

**Sequence adjustment:** the structure above follows the instruction's expected sequence unchanged; no repository authority found in this research requires a different order. The one open sequencing sub-question (drafting subscription-terms content now vs. after the full `DEC-SUB-*` plan catalogue is confirmed — Founder Decision Sheet FD-7) sits inside step 1/7 and does not change the ten-step order itself.

**What this plan does not do:** it does not move `DEC-LEGAL-002` or `EXT-LEG-002` through any of the above steps. It is a sequence description only, produced so the Founder can authorize each step explicitly.
