`DEC-LEGAL-002`'s own Decision Register entry, the Decision Resolution Plan, the Master Workflow, and the Coding-Agent Prompt Register all consistently frame it as a **Phase 14/15 pilot-gate** dependency (Priority D3 — "before pilot/launch"). ## Phase B — The Current Product Model (factual description for legal counsel)

### B.1 Platform

- **What 11thONUS provides:** a shared, multi-tenant SaaS platform on which independent businesses each configure and run their own reward ("loyalty") programme. The platform supplies identity, business/staff account structure, a shared "Commerce Knowledge" catalogue of purchasable categories, purchase recording and verification (Verified Units), reward-cycle mechanics, and (subject to open commercial decisions) a paid subscription relationship with businesses. Source: [11thONUS-at-a-Glance](../../../11thONUS-at-a-Glance.md); [Product Manifesto](../../../01-product/11thONUS%20Product%20Manifesto.md).
- **What 11thONUS records/verifies:** business and staff identity; purchase events recorded against a business's Commerce Knowledge catalogue; Verified Units accumulated per customer per Reward Program; Reward Program configuration set by each business; Terms acceptance records (who accepted which Terms version, when, in which language) — see Phase G.
- **What it does not control:** each business's own Reward Program design (what is rewarded, thresholds, reward value), the underlying goods/services a business sells or delivers, or the business's operational relationship with its own customers. Per the [11thONUS-at-a-Glance](../../../11thONUS-at-a-Glance.md) "11thONUS Promise": *"We believe the platform should standardise trust—not standardise how businesses build customer relationships."*
- **Relationship between platform, participating business, and customer:** the platform is the common infrastructure and trust layer; each business is an independent tenant that owns its own Reward Program and customer relationship; the customer participates in a specific business's programme through the shared platform. Per the [Product Manifesto](../../../01-product/11thONUS%20Product%20Manifesto.md) §"We Believe Every Business Owns Its Customer Relationship": *"11thONUS supports the relationship. It does not take ownership of it."*

### B.2 Business

- **How a business joins:** registers on the platform (Capability 3 / `ENG-P2-002`, `ENG-P3-002`), establishes its owner/staff structure (Capability 3 / `ENG-P2-003`), selects from the shared Commerce Knowledge seed catalogue (`ENG-P3-001`), and proceeds through an onboarding flow (`ENG-P3-002` Packages A–H) that ends with `submitBusinessForVerification`.
- **What activation/verification means:** a business moves from a draft/setup state to `pending_verification` only after (a) all onboarding data is complete and (b) the business owner has accepted the **current governed Terms version** — enforced server-side, transactionally, by `assertCurrentBusinessTermsAccepted` (see Phase G). Verification itself (the platform's review before a business goes fully live) is a separate step not further detailed in the sources reviewed for this brief.
- **Who controls its own Reward Program:** the business. Per `DEC-SUB-007` (CONFIRMED), essential trust controls — customer verification, staff identities, purchase history, Verified Unit integrity, redemption controls, dispute handling, basic audit, privacy/security — are present in every commercial plan; the platform does not gate these behind price tier, but it also does not design or approve the content of any individual business's Reward Program.
- **What obligations arise from publishing/offering a Reward Program:** programme *publication* itself remains open (see Business Obligation Matrix row "Programme publication," still `PRODUCT DECISION REQUIRED`). But two specific obligations are now Founder-positioned (2026-08-29, `DEC-LEGAL-002-FOUNDER-DISP-001`): a Business's earned-reward obligation survives its own suspension and exit (FD-2, FD-3), and a Business may change its programme only prospectively, never retrospectively removing or materially reducing an already-earned reward (FD-5). These are product/commercial positions, not legal conclusions — legal form, exceptions, and remedies remain for counsel.
- **What the platform currently expects regarding honouring earned/valid rewards:** **Founder position recorded (FD-2, 2026-08-29):** suspension does not, by itself, extinguish rewards validly earned before suspension; the Business's obligation to honour them survives, subject to the applicable Reward Program terms and any circumstances where fulfilment is legally impossible or counsel advises otherwise. **`DEC-LOY-011` resolved (2026-08-29, same task, now CONFIRMED):** the previously separate redemption-mechanism question is settled as Option (a) — redeemable by default during suspension, subject to governed exceptions (fraud, security/integrity, legal/regulatory, disputed validity, or another governed exception); commercial-relationship/subscription-status suspension alone does not block redemption; manual review is not the default. See Founder Decision Sheet FD-2 and Business Obligation Matrix row "Honouring valid rewards."

### B.3 Customer

- **How participation works:** a customer transacts with a specific business; the business (or its staff) records the qualifying purchase against the business's Commerce Knowledge categories.
- **How eligible purchases/loyalty progress are recorded:** as Verified Units, per the [Verified Loyalty Principles](../../verified-loyalty-principles.md) — Verified Units are "the only unit of account" and are processed immediately, with no pending state; a customer has at most one actively-accumulating Loyalty Cycle per Reward Program at a time.
- **How rewards become available:** a Loyalty Cycle completes when its threshold is met, producing a Reward. A Reward's quantity is fixed at creation. Multiple completed-cycle Rewards may coexist unredeemed for the same customer.
- **How redemption works:** governed by the Verified Loyalty Principles as a shared responsibility between customer and merchant ("merchant redemption-assistance is a shared responsibility"); the specific consumer-facing mechanics beyond this principle-level statement were not located as CONFIRMED product authority in this research pass and are not asserted here.
- **Whether rewards have monetary/cash value:** **Founder position recorded (FD-6, 2026-08-29):** a reward is a benefit offered under the applicable Business's Reward Program — not money held by 11thONUS, not a stored cash balance, and not a general cash-withdrawal entitlement; unless a governed Reward Program expressly permits otherwise, rewards are not redeemable for cash and are fulfilled by the Business. Not recorded: that rewards have "no monetary value" — a reward may plainly have economic value without being cash. This does not prejudge separately governed future products (gift cards, stored value, other instruments). Legal characterisation and required disclosures remain for counsel — see Founder Decision Sheet FD-6.
- **What happens when programme conditions change:** expiry is "opt-in per-Reward-Program only, never automatic or applied to Verified Units/progress" per the Verified Loyalty Principles. **Founder position recorded (FD-5, 2026-08-29):** beyond expiry, a Business may change its Reward Program prospectively (subject to applicable programme terms, platform trust requirements, and applicable law), but must not retrospectively remove or materially reduce a reward already earned under the rules in force when it was earned. Required notice, effective timing, consumer-protection requirements, and permissible exceptions remain for counsel — see Founder Decision Sheet FD-5.

### B.4 The 11thONUS boundary principle — confirmed against authoritative sources

The instruction's stated principle — *"11thONUS standardizes trust; it does not standardize how businesses build or manage customer relationships"* — is **confirmed**, with its most precise wording traced to [11thONUS-at-a-Glance](../../../11thONUS-at-a-Glance.md) (*"The platform standardises trust, not how businesses build customer relationships"*) and independently corroborated in substance (not verbatim) by the [Product Manifesto](../../../01-product/11thONUS%20Product%20Manifesto.md) §"We Believe Every Business Owns Its Customer Relationship" (*"11thONUS supports the relationship. It does not take ownership of it,"* and *"a platform that owns the customer relationship"* is listed under "What We Will Not Become"). Both sources are consistent; neither is contradicted by any governance or decision-register document reviewed. This brief and its companions rely on this principle as confirmed product authority — no shared, platform-wide loyalty programme is assumed or proposed anywhere in this preparation work.
