---

**Version 1.0**

# Mission

Knowledge Studio is the editorial platform responsible for governing the Commerce Knowledge Layer.

Its purpose is to ensure that every business, Reward Program, product, service, category and tag used throughout 11thONUS remains accurate, consistent, multilingual and ready for future intelligence.

Knowledge Studio protects one of the platform's most valuable long-term assets: its commercial knowledge.

---

# Design Philosophy

Businesses should never spend time creating categories that already exist.

Customers should never struggle to find businesses because similar services were classified differently.

Knowledge should be curated once and reused everywhere.

---

# Knowledge Versioning

Knowledge evolves.

Historical references must remain valid.

Every knowledge object therefore includes:

- Version
- Effective Date
- Status
- Replacement Reference (if retired)

This ensures historical Reward Programs remain understandable even as the taxonomy evolves.

# Architectural Principles

1. **Curate Once, Reuse Everywhere**
    
    Commercial knowledge is maintained centrally and reused by every module.
    
2. **Standardise Before You Customise**
    
    Businesses select from trusted standards first, then personalise how they present themselves to customers.
    
3. **Multilingual by Design**
    
    Every knowledge object supports translation and localisation.
    
4. **Version Without Breaking History**
    
    Taxonomy evolves without invalidating historical Reward Programs, reports or customer activity.
    
5. **Human-Governed, AI-Assisted**
    
    AI helps editors work faster, but people remain responsible for approving changes.
